<h2 align="center">Install or upgrade</h2>

<p align="center">
  You need a Steam Frame with internet access and 12 GiB of free storage,<br>
  plus your own authorized <strong>original Xbox Halo CE ISO/XISO or extracted maps</strong>.<br>
  USA Rev 2 was validated with the stable installer. PC, Anniversary and MCC data are unsupported.
</p>

<p align="center">
  <strong>1. Run the EXE and choose Install or Repair.</strong><br>
  Select your Xbox game data for a first install.<br>
  A verified 1.3.5 installation can reuse its maps without another ISO.
</p>

<p align="center">
  <strong>2. Connect your Frame over USB-C or network SSH.</strong><br>
  Enable Developer Mode, set the Frame's user password,<br>
  and follow the installer's connection guide.
</p>

<p align="center">
  <strong>3. Save and close games, then start installation.</strong><br>
  Keep Steam Home and SteamVR running, and leave the Frame awake and connected.<br>
  Building on the Frame can take tens of minutes.
</p>

<p align="center">
  <strong>1.4.0 replaces 1.3.5 in place</strong> and keeps <strong>Halo: Combat Evolved VR (Native)</strong> in Steam.<br>
  Your old saves remain unchanged. Older checkpoints are incompatible with the new engine;<br>
  create a <strong>new profile and campaign</strong> after upgrading.
</p>
