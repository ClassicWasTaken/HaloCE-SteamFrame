Play native ARM64/OpenXR Halo CE on Steam Frame with **LAN and online campaign co-op**, **head-directed walking**, and **Xbox-style buttons**. Tracked controller aiming, a render-rate reticle, half-strength stereo sun glow, and automatic Steam artwork and Notes are included.

## Install or upgrade

You need a Steam Frame with internet access and **12 GiB of free storage**, plus your own authorized **original Xbox Halo CE ISO/XISO or extracted maps**. USA Rev 2 was validated with the stable installer. PC, Anniversary and MCC data are unsupported.

1. **Run the EXE and choose Install or Repair.** Select your Xbox game data for a first install. A verified 1.3.5 installation can reuse its maps without another ISO.
2. **Connect your Frame over USB-C or network SSH.** Enable Developer Mode, set the Frame's user password, and follow the installer's connection guide.
3. **Save and close games, then start installation.** Keep Steam Home and SteamVR running, and leave the Frame awake and connected. Building on the Frame can take tens of minutes.

**1.4.0 replaces 1.3.5 in place** and keeps **Halo: Combat Evolved VR (Native)** in Steam. Your old saves remain unchanged. Older checkpoints are incompatible with the new engine; create a **new profile and campaign** after upgrading.
