# Halo CE Steam Frame VR Mod 1.4.2

[**Download Halo-Steam-Frame-Mod-Setup-1.4.2.exe**](https://github.com/ClassicWasTaken/HaloSteamFrameMod/releases/download/v1.4.2/Halo-Steam-Frame-Mod-Setup-1.4.2.exe) for Windows 10/11 x64. This is the release's only installer asset. Its SHA256 is listed on the [release page](https://github.com/ClassicWasTaken/HaloSteamFrameMod/releases/tag/v1.4.2); matching installer source is in the [v1.4.2 tag](https://github.com/ClassicWasTaken/HaloSteamFrameMod/tree/v1.4.2). [Verify release provenance](BUILD_PROVENANCE.md).

- **SD-card installation:** choose Internal storage or SD card on Connect Frame, click **Refresh storage**, and select the detected card. Install, Repair and Uninstall use the selected location. SD cards need a mounted, writable, executable **ext4 or f2fs** filesystem and **12 GiB free**. Setup does not format or mount cards. Missing or changed cards stop the operation; other installations are kept. [SD-card guide](SD_CARD.md).
- **Separate SD library entry:** **Halo: Combat Evolved VR (Native, SD card)** coexists with the internal **Halo: Combat Evolved VR (Native)**. A new SD installation starts separate profiles and campaign saves in its own `save-v1.4`. There is no automatic campaign migration between drives. Steam artwork and Notes stay with your Steam account; Podman's container storage can still use internal space.
- **Menus in front of you:** opening a menu uses your current headset position and gaze. It stays in place while open after walking or turning; closing and reopening captures your new view. Recentring deliberately places an open menu in front again.
- **Cancellation and progress:** a completed user cancellation reports Setup cancelled. Genuine failures, unconfirmed remote stopping and failed disconnect cleanup still need attention. Password redaction no longer interferes with progress-phase lookup. Fixes [#10](https://github.com/ClassicWasTaken/HaloSteamFrameMod/issues/10) and [#12](https://github.com/ClassicWasTaken/HaloSteamFrameMod/issues/12).
- **Steam choices preserved:** re-registering an existing shortcut keeps hidden status, desktop configuration, overlay and devkit choices. The VR-library flag remains enabled. Fixes [#11](https://github.com/ClassicWasTaken/HaloSteamFrameMod/issues/11).
- **Safe repair and upgrade recovery:** malformed or unsupported configuration is rejected with readable preservation messages. An interrupted 1.3.5 upgrade can resume when the owned cache, original backups and replacement files provide consistent proof. Keep those records; damaged or ambiguous evidence requires manual recovery. Fixes [#13](https://github.com/ClassicWasTaken/HaloSteamFrameMod/issues/13) and [#18](https://github.com/ClassicWasTaken/HaloSteamFrameMod/issues/18).
- **Bounded SSH waits:** command and SFTP setup handshakes can be cancelled and have deadlines. Continuous or newline-free output cannot indefinitely postpone cancellation or grow its retained buffer. Fixes [#19](https://github.com/ClassicWasTaken/HaloSteamFrameMod/issues/19) and [#20](https://github.com/ClassicWasTaken/HaloSteamFrameMod/issues/20).
- **Verified USB tools first:** an explicit adb.exe selection takes priority, followed by the verified bundled tools. SDK/PATH discovery is a fallback when the bundle is absent; a damaged bundle fails closed. This does not authenticate an already-running ADB server. Fixes [#21](https://github.com/ClassicWasTaken/HaloSteamFrameMod/issues/21).

**Updating:** save and close Halo, select its storage location, then choose Repair to rebuild using verified Xbox maps without another ISO. Existing 1.4.0/1.4.1 profiles and checkpoints on that location stay in `save-v1.4`. The 1.4 engine **cannot load old checkpoints** from 1.3.5: that upgrade retains old saves and backs up the original config/program, but requires a new profile and campaign. Separate 1.4.0a2–a5 Experimental installations stay untouched.

The [1.4.1 tutorial, tracking and unarmed-flashlight fixes](https://github.com/ClassicWasTaken/HaloSteamFrameMod/releases/tag/v1.4.1) remain included, with standing mode and snap turning applied on Install/Repair. Source stays pinned to `2ae0ee4e3e8a4dfdadfd528a5b085ca699fc9ea4`, native peer protocol **17**. LAN/online campaign, head-directed walking, Xbox buttons, render-rate reticle and half-strength stereo sun glow are retained. Use matching builds/maps for co-op; retail clients cannot join. Restrictive NAT can prevent Internet connections because there is no gameplay relay.

The user confirmed the private build's SD installation and menu placement on their headset. Earlier testing confirmed the 1.4.0 baseline's reticle motion and LAN/online campaign. No measured FPS, frame timing or complete new multiplayer/hardware matrix was supplied. See [release provenance](BUILD_PROVENANCE.md) for verification of the exact source commit, build and downloaded artifact.

Supply your own authorized original Xbox Halo CE data. USA Rev 2 was validated with the stable installer. No retail executable, ISO, maps, keys or soundtrack is bundled or downloaded. Halo artwork and licensed components retain their notices. The EXE is unsigned; use the checksum and provenance verification instructions to check its source and artifact.
