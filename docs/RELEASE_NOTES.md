# Halo CE Steam Frame VR Mod 1.4.0

[**Download Halo-Steam-Frame-Mod-Setup-1.4.0.exe**](https://github.com/ClassicWasTaken/HaloSteamFrameMod/releases/download/v1.4.0/Halo-Steam-Frame-Mod-Setup-1.4.0.exe) for Windows 10/11 x64. This is the release's only installer asset. Its SHA256 is listed on the [release page](https://github.com/ClassicWasTaken/HaloSteamFrameMod/releases/tag/v1.4.0); matching installer source is in the [v1.4.0 tag](https://github.com/ClassicWasTaken/HaloSteamFrameMod/tree/v1.4.0). The headset tester confirmed the engine and controls used by this release, including reticle motion and LAN/online campaign co-op.

- **Render-rate reticle:** corrects the visible stepping reported after 1.4.0a4. The read-only shot preview uses current-frame aim for the local on-foot player instead of only the 30 Hz unit direction. It retains the shared muzzle/camera calculation, trigger offsets, aim assist, collision mask and current hit test. Actual shots, spread and ballistics keep their behavior. Vehicle aiming retains the bounded turret path. The tester confirmed the new motion on the headset; no frame-time or FPS measurements were supplied.
- **LAN and online campaign:** Multiplayer > CO-OP CAMPAIGN offers HOST LOCAL (LAN), JOIN LOCAL (LAN), HOST ONLINE (INTERNET), JOIN ONLINE (INTERNET), and JOIN INVITE LINK. Campaign uses one local player per machine in VR and flat mode; the former second-controller/split-screen campaign route is removed. Campaign hosting starts on SINGLEPLAYER maps. Competitive multiplayer keeps its existing routes.
- **Updated Steam Notes:** Install/Repair adds the new co-op directions automatically. Only exact unchanged previous installer notes are upgraded; edited notes and other user fields are preserved.
- **Earlier fixes retained:** nominal shot/reticle alignment, half-strength stereo sun glow, head-directed movement, Xbox-style buttons and physical-head tutorial controls. The tester reported that 1.4.0a4 corrected aiming alignment, then confirmed the updated reticle motion and campaign routes work in the build used for 1.4.0.

**1.4.0 replaces 1.3.5 in place.** Close the native game and choose **Install** or **Repair**. Install recognizes a verified 1.3.5 installation and rebuilds it; verified Xbox maps can be reused without another ISO. The game remains at `~/Games/HaloCENativeVR` with the Steam title **Halo: Combat Evolved VR (Native)** and the normal installer cache.

Old 1.3.5 saves remain unchanged in their original `save` folder or configured location. The original config and program files are backed up in the owned installer run's `previous-program-files` folder. The new engine uses `~/Games/HaloCENativeVR/save-v1.4`: create a new profile and campaign. Its larger game-state layout cannot load old checkpoints, and setup does not automatically migrate checkpoints or profiles. Existing 1.4.0a2–a5 copies under `~/Games/HaloCENativeVRExperimental` remain untouched and can be kept for reference; this public installer targets the normal native installation.

Source remains pinned to `2ae0ee4e3e8a4dfdadfd528a5b085ca699fc9ea4`, native peer protocol **17**. Use matching builds/maps for co-op; stable protocol 11 and retail Halo clients cannot join. Online lobbies stay private by default; public listing lets anyone join. Internet play has no gameplay relay, so restrictive NAT can prevent distant peers from connecting. The reported headset test does not specify peer distance or prove compatibility across all networks. Renderer changes do not establish the cause or resolution of earlier white flashes or stuttering; actual Frame performance has not been measured.

Supply your own authorized original Xbox Halo CE data. No retail executable, ISO, maps, keys, or soundtrack is bundled or downloaded. Halo identification artwork and licensed components retain their notices. The EXE is unsigned; verify it against the SHA256 shown on the release page.
